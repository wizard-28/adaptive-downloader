# Data terms (proposed; subject to ownership verification)

The MIT license in `LICENSE` applies to the **source code** only.

Experimental data (`testdata/`: raw run records, manifests, analysis outputs,
figures, reports) is proposed to be released under **Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)** *once the data owners confirm they
hold the rights to do so*.

Not covered by the proposed terms and retaining their original licenses:

- recorded cellular traces and any other third-party trace files (consult the
  trace providers' terms before redistribution);
- Mahimahi (a separate project with its own license; it is not vendored here);
- aria2 output text captured in run records is machine output of aria2 1.37.0.

Before publication the authors must confirm: (1) who holds copyright in the code
and data, (2) that every contributor agrees to the MIT license, and (3) the
terms of each third-party dataset. The copyright line in `LICENSE` and the
author list in `CITATION.cff` are placeholders until confirmed.
