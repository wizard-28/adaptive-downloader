Thank you. Questions?

WHAT TO SAY: To sum up in one sentence: changing connections and piece sizes during a download pays off only when using more connections has a cost that changes over time, and even then, getting the download engine right matters more than the controller. Fixed settings cannot adapt, but in the conditions we tested they mostly did not need to. We are preparing a paper for the journal IEEE Access; all its numbers are checked against our raw data. Thank you, and we are happy to take questions. IN SIMPLE WORDS: Adapting is not automatically better. Measure whether there is something to gain first, and fix the basics before adding intelligence. TERMS ON THIS SLIDE: • IEEE Access: an open-access engineering journal that judges papers on soundness. • Engine details: the basic downloading machinery (timeouts, straggler splitting), as opposed to the controller that decides N and S.
