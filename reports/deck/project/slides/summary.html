WHAT TO SAY: This chart puts the main comparisons together. Each row is one adaptive or AI controller against a fixed setting. The dot is the time ratio and the line is the uncertainty range. Left of the dashed line means the controller was faster. The green ones, where adapting won, are all against fixed settings that use the same small pieces, or measured on our old engine. The red ones, where adapting lost, are against the best fixed setting with a sound engine. IN SIMPLE WORDS: Adapting wins only against a weaker fixed choice. Against the best fixed choice, it lost every time we tried. TERMS ON THIS SLIDE: • Small-piece fixed setting: a fixed setting using the same 1 MiB pieces as the controller, for example 16 × 1 MiB. • 16 × 32 MiB: the best fixed setting on the throttling server, 16 connections with 32 MiB pieces. • Old engine: our downloader before the two fixes (time limit, straggler splitting).
