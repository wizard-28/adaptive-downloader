WHAT TO SAY: What is new? First, what is not new: other people have built downloaders that tune the number of connections, and download managers already split slow pieces. We do not claim a new algorithm. Our contribution is answering when adapting is worth it at all, and explaining why. We measured the most any controller could gain before training any AI. We explained most of a 62 % gap against aria2 as a timing effect, and we say what we could not explain. We showed that two engine details mattered more than any controller; to our knowledge, earlier download-tuning work does not separate engine from controller like this. We found that a hidden server rule (a request keeps its starting speed) drives the big-piece wins, and tested it with a control server. And we report every AI agent honestly, with its limits, including tests on real servers. IN SIMPLE WORDS: Others asked “how do we tune it?”. We asked “is there anything to tune?”, measured it carefully, and explained the answer. TERMS ON THIS SLIDE: • Mechanism: the cause behind a result. • Confounder: a hidden factor that affects results and can be mistaken for something else. • Falcon, direct search: other published methods that tune the number of parallel connections while transferring. • Raw artifacts: the original run records and analysis files our numbers are recomputed from.
